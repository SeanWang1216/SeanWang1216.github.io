Experimental Instructions & Game Demo

Strategic Complexity in Decomposed Games · Yu-Hsiang (Sean) Wang

Demonstration version

This demonstration contains one Aggregated round and one Decomposed round. It is for learning and illustration only: there is no real payment, no data collection, and the other player is a clearly marked simulated opponent. Your role is fixed across both rounds.

You will first read the original Aggregated instructions, complete the comprehension quiz, make one decision, then read the original Decomposed instructions, complete its quiz, and make one decision.

Original Aggregated Instructions

Section 1 of 12

Roles

You are randomly assigned to one of two roles: A or B. Your role will remain fixed throughout the entire experiment.

In each task, you will be randomly matched with another participant who has the opposite role from you. You and the participant you are matched with will each choose one of two options, and your earnings will depend on both players' choices. You will not be told the other participant's choice or your earnings until the end of the experiment.

Task Overview

This part of the experiment consists of 2 tasks.
In each task, you and the participant you are matched with will see the same table.
The table varies across tasks, but the format is the same.

Here is an example of what a table looks like:
